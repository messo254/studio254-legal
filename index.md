# Privacy Policy for Studio254

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

Studio254 ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Studio254** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our App and services.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you create an account and use Studio254, we collect only the information necessary to provide our fitness, nutrition, and membership services:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and encrypted login credentials.
- **Fitness & Progress Data:** Workout logs, training history, fitness goals, body metrics (weight, height, age, gender), step counts, and progress photos you voluntarily upload.
- **Nutrition & Dietary Data:** Meal logs, food items, serving quantities, calorie totals, macronutrient breakdowns (protein, carbohydrates, and fat), daily nutrition targets, and optional meal photos you voluntarily upload.
- **Camera & Photo Library Access:** Requested strictly on-demand when you choose to scan a packaged food barcode or capture/attach a progress or meal photo. Live camera frames used for barcode scanning are processed locally on your device to read the barcode number and are never recorded or uploaded.
- **Payment & Subscription Information:** Subscription tier, transaction status, and M-Pesa payment reference numbers processed via Safaricom Daraja API. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Basic network status and error logs used to maintain offline sync and app stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your Studio254 member account.
- Track your workouts, progress photos, daily calories, macronutrients, and fitness milestones.
- Calculate personalized calorie and macro targets (via the Mifflin-St Jeor formula) and look up nutritional facts for scanned barcodes.
- Process membership subscriptions and verify M-Pesa payments.
- Sync offline activity when your device reconnects to the internet.
- Communicate important service updates and respond to support requests.

## 3. Data Storage, Security & Zero AI Training
Your data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secure cloud servers. Authentication tokens and passwords are encrypted and protected using industry-standard security controls.

Studio254 strictly prohibits using your personal, fitness, biometric, or nutrition data (including meal logs, workout history, and uploaded photos) to train, fine-tune, or benchmark artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We share limited, necessary data only with trusted service providers required to operate the App:
- **Safaricom (M-Pesa Daraja API):** Phone number and payment amount to process STK Push subscription payments.
- **Open Food Facts (`world.openfoodfacts.org`):** When you scan or enter a product barcode in the Nutrition Tracker, only the numeric barcode string is sent to the public Open Food Facts API to retrieve nutritional facts. No user ID, name, email, or personal health data is shared.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** Encrypted database and server hosting.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, dietary logs, or fitness data to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain your personal, fitness, and nutrition data for as long as your account remains active. Deleting an individual meal log or progress photo permanently deletes the associated image file from our servers.

You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and all associated records (including workouts, meal logs, nutrition targets, progress photos, and meal photos) at any time from the App in your **Profile** tab (**Settings → Clear Data/Delete Account**) or by contacting us at **studio.254@icloud.com**.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Studio254 is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

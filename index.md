# Privacy Policy for Studio254

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

Studio254 ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Studio254** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our App and services.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you create an account and use Studio254, we collect only the information necessary to provide our fitness and membership services:
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and encrypted login credentials.
- **Fitness & Progress Data:** Workout logs, training history, fitness goals, and progress photos you voluntarily upload.
- **Payment & Subscription Information:** Subscription tier, transaction status, and M-Pesa payment reference numbers processed via Safaricom Daraja API. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN.
- **Device & Diagnostic Data:** Basic network status and error logs used to maintain offline sync and app stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Create and manage your Studio254 member account.
- Track your workouts, progress photos, and fitness milestones.
- Process membership subscriptions and verify M-Pesa payments.
- Sync offline activity when your device reconnects to the internet.
- Communicate important service updates and respond to support requests.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
Your data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secure cloud servers. Authentication tokens and passwords are encrypted and protected using industry-standard security controls.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We share limited, necessary data only with trusted service providers required to operate the App:
- **Safaricom (M-Pesa Daraja API):** Phone number and payment amount to process STK Push subscription payments.
- **Cloud Infrastructure Providers:** Encrypted database and server hosting.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or fitness data to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion
We retain your personal and fitness data for as long as your account remains active. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at **[YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL]**.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Studio254 is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
